## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Icelandic buyers comparing used vehicles on phones; owners seeking cash purchase or consignment. The user's confirmed priority is extremely clear navigation, search, vehicle information and efficient use of space.

## Personality and reference
Confident, direct, helpful. Preserve Claude's Bílás photography and typography. Improve its distant search, weak categories and external detail handoffs. Accessible light/dark themes required by Sindri.

## Principles
Make inventory reachable immediately. Make equivalent facts comparable. Show provenance and limitations honestly. Keep mobile controls stable and usable. Put the correct next action beside the relevant information.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast target, semantic headings and controls, keyboard navigation, focus management, 44px targets, reduced motion, no hover-only information.
