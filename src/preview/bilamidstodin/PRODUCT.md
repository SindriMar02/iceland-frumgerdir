# Bílamiðstöðin

## Register
product

## Audience and purpose
Icelandic vehicle buyers finding suitable cars, SUVs, work vehicles and recreational stock; owners seeking cash purchase or consignment. Improve selection and the detail supplied in an enquiry.

## Reference and intent
Reuse the working Bílás-derived showroom system with white default, optional saved dark mode, real Bílamiðstöðin identity, photos, stock, published staff/services/fees. The user wants a commercial gap bridged, not a cosmetic promise.

## Principles
Inventory first. Budget and use case before hundreds of options. Comparable specs and price basis always visible. No invented live stock, loan rates, guaranteed purchase or reviews. Keep bilasolur.is inventory workflow possible; approved integration is a gate before production.

## Accessibility
AA text contrast, 44px targets, native mobile scroll, keyboard/focus containment, reduced motion and readable price/vehicle information.
