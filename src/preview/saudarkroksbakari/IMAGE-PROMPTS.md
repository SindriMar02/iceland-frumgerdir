# Sauðárkróksbakarí — Seedream 4.5 image prompt pack (bright)

**Direction:** "Morgunninn á Króknum" — one consistent bright, warm, editorial morning
light across the whole page. Pale Carrara-marble surfaces, soft natural daylight, warm
cream / espresso-brown / natural-pine palette. No dark/noir frames — every shot is airy.

**How to generate:** Higgsfield **web app**, **Seedream 4.5**, **Unlimited toggle ON** (zero
credits — do NOT use the MCP, it always burns credits).

**Where to put them:** save each result into
`iceland-redesigns/public/saudarkroksbakari/<filename>.jpg` using the EXACT filename listed.
The page already points at these paths and shows a graceful cream gradient until the file exists.

> **Fact-check rule:** these are prototype placeholders for items the bakery is *sourced* to sell
> (snúður in 3 glazes, the pink-iced donut, the Nutella "langjón", Danish rúgbrauð, vegan
> croissant, coffee). Do NOT generate kransakaka, hálfmánar, randalín, marengs, etc. — no source
> ties them to this bakery. Replace all of these with the client's real photography before launch.

## Shared mood suffix (append to EVERY prompt below)

> Hyper-realistic photograph, bright Nordic morning: soft natural daylight on a pale
> Carrara-marble surface, airy and clean, warm cream / espresso-brown / natural-pine palette.
> Medium-format prime lens, shallow depth of field, true-to-life colour, gentle film grain,
> refined editorial quality. **Avoid:** CGI or plastic look, oversaturation, HDR halos, dark
> or moody low-key lighting, distorted or fake text/letters, watermark, brand logos, deformed
> hands, AI artifacts.
