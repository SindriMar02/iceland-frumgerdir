# Vatt prototype

Register: brand (a dealer site, design is the pitch). Spec prototype for Vatt ehf, the Icelandic importer of BYD and Maxus (OMODA, EXLANTIX and JAECOO from January 2027). Audience: private EV buyers and fleet buyers in Iceland, reading on a phone first. Job of the page: show the six brands under one roof with real prices, and let a visitor book a test drive or a workshop slot without calling. Facts only from `_docs/VATT-FACTCHECK-2026-10-08.md` and the harvest. Design system and the reasoning: `DESIGN.md`.
