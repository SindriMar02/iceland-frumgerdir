Rule learned the hard way on this project: never render ð-words in text-transform:uppercase unless the font's capital Eth is verified — some fonts fall back to a bar-less "D". Cabinet Grotesk's uppercase Ð/Þ are correct (see above), but double-check on every new font before shipping an all-caps label.
